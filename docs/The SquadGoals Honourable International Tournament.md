# The SquadGoals Honourable International Tournament (the SHIT)

# 1\. Schedule

This event occurs every week on a Tuesday, starting at 20:00 UTC+1, and continues for approximately 1.5 hours following that time. During the period when the event is active, participants may join and leave both the lobby and the text channel as they please.

# 2\. Format

2.1 The event would take the form of a customs lobby hosted by one of the organizers (“the Referee”), with all participants playing simultaneously. Each show would run until its completion.

2.2 The Referee will be chosen and made public by the organizers on the day of the event. The Referee can choose whether to participate competitively in the games \- in such case, points will be assigned to them like to any other player. Alternatively should the Referee choose not to participate, they will eliminate themselves as soon as possible following the start of each show, spectating all participants to ensure rules are being adhered to and that points can be accurately tracked. They will keep account of the points that each player receives after each show is finished, and progressively calculate the total of points that each player is achieving. They will also be in charge of applying disciplinary measures towards other players if needed.

2.3 At the date and time specified in the previous section, the Referee will share the customs lobby code necessary to join within the channel assigned to the event. It is to be noted that this code is only for Squad Goals members and should not be shared outside said server.

2.4 The shows played on the event day will be, in this order:

* Hex-a-Thon / X-treme Solos  
* Thin Ice / Party Time Tumble  
* Vault  
* Jump Around  
* Roll Call  
* Xtreme Solos  
* Blast Ball Trials  
* Solos  
* Blast Ball Trials  
* Jump Around  
* Roll Call / Party Time Tumble (the latter, if there are more than 20 players)  
* Blast Ball Trials

When the show is not available due to the player count exceeding its upper limit or not reaching its lower limit it will be replaced by the first available show appearing on the same line after the slash, if there is one. If no replacement show has been established after a slash, then it shall be substituted with The Vault instead. If the show that needs to be replaced is The Vault, then it will be replaced by Solos shows.

2.5 The event will end at the Referee’s discretion, not necessarily at the end of this list; and if the Referee judges it convenient, he might start replaying the list after it has finished.

2.6 Communication between all participants takes place within channel \#shit-text-chat of the Squad Goals server for the duration of the event.

# 3\. Forbidden Actions

Each player is expected to compete individually \- this is in the interest of keeping the event fair for all participants. If the Referee discovers that a player has engaged in any forbidden action during the event then they could, at their discretion, issue a warning to the player or sanction them with a deduction of points, exclusion from a certain amount (or all) remaining shows for that week’s event and/or exclusion from future weeks of the event.

3.1 **Collusion and alliances** (also known as ‘teaming’) with other players are not allowed. Collusion or an alliance is deemed to have occurred when there is a reasonable belief that a group of two or more players have a premeditated agreement to play in a way that is intended to be either beneficial to one or more players within that group, or particularly detrimental to one or more players outside of that group. It includes, but is not limited to, double grabs, double pushes, coordinated attacks with bombs/blast balls and in general any kind of joint actions with the purpose of damaging or killing a specific player.

3.2 **Piggybacks** are not permitted. Neither being carried by another player nor giving a piggyback to another player is allowed. Should players accidentally initiate a piggyback, they must dismount or throw off the person they are carrying immediately \- failing to do so will result in both players involved in the piggyback being disallowed from earning points during the show in which the piggyback occurred, and both players will be required to eliminate themselves as soon as possible (either by throwing themselves into slime/water or by failing to cross the finish line).

3.3 Stepping, standing, bouncing or jumping on **invisible surfaces** or floors which are not meant to officially be part of the map or using them in any other way is not permitted. In the particular case of the invisible floor that lies above part of the final Royal Fumble, any player who steps on it will be automatically disqualified and not allowed to win the final. In the cases of invisible surfaces on other maps the referee has the power to warn or sanction the player that has used them in the way they consider appropriate for the case, including disqualification for the rest of the round or the event, particularly if the infringement persists.

# 4\. Scoring

4.1 For each show played, the general rule (see exceptions below) is that the last 3 survivors in the finals will receive points as follows:

* First place \- 3 points  
* Second place \- 2 points  
* Third place \- 1 point  
* All other players will receive zero points.

In the cases of finals where players need to grab a crown (Fall Mountain and Lost Temple) or a tail (Royal Fumble), or races which work as finals (for example, Lily Leapers), 2 points will be assigned to the winner and zero to all other players.

If more than one player grabs the crown and gets the win simultaneously, 2 points will be awarded to each player grabbing the crown. In these cases, screenshots will be appreciated but are not mandatory; the Referee will determine the winners based on what he saw.

Similarly, if the last remaining players in a final round are eliminated at the same time, resulting in a screen showing “0 players remaining” rather than the usual celebration of the winner, all of those last remaining players who were eliminated simultaneously will be awarded 2 points each.

If any duos show (including, but not limited to, Duos and Blast Ball Bangers) were featured at the event, each of the winners will be awarded 2 points, while the rest of the teams won’t be awarded any, no matter the order in which they lost.

4.2 The winner in any given week of the event would then be the participant who gains the most points across all shows played during that week. A running total of each player’s points for all weeks would also be maintained by the organizers (purely for informational purposes).

4.3 Load of SHIT: Every week the winner of the previous week shall start with a handicap score of minus two (-2) points. This negative score is cumulative in case of consecutive wins, meaning that someone who wins 2 consecutive weeks shall start the following week with minus four (-4) points, someone who wins 3 consecutive weeks shall start the following week with minus six (-6) points, and so on. The handicap is reset to zero once the player fails to reach first place at the end of a week.

This handicap will not be taken into account in the overall score board.

4.4 Three Bounty rule: during the first 6 shows of each week, killing the winner of the previous week in any round in which players are eliminated by falling once into the slime will award 2 points to the player who made the kill, subject to the following:

1) As a general reference, a kill will be a grab, a release, a baiting, a push, a joust, or a blast ball throw that causes the previous winner’s elimination within the following 3 seconds of that action.  
2) The player who performs the kill must survive the incident (in other words, points won’t be awarded for suicide kills).  
3) When the kill can be attributed to the circumstantial and spontaneous action of two different players, the referee has the right, at their discretion, to split the bounty and give 1 point to each of those players.  
4) In cases where a kill is contested, the Referee will make the ultimate decision as to who is eligible to be awarded points for the kill, or even whether an action is deemed to be a kill or not.

This rule will only be valid during the first 6 shows of each week, and it will apply to the first 3 kills against the winner of the previous week. After any of those two conditions is met, any kill that the winner of the previous week suffers won’t award points to the player who killed him. Any kills against the winner of the previous week, whether points are awarded or not, will count against this 3-kill limit.

4.5 Jump Showdown: in the case of Jump Showdown, if the remaining players alive are on different platforms which are on opposite sides of the central post (in other words, in an angle of 180° from each other), the player who performs a successful bar jump to reach the other platform will get an extra 3 points. This rule shall only become applicable to a player when the only way he has of reaching the remaining player or players is performing a bar jump.

If the player attempting the barjump fails to reach the other platform but still manages to bounce upwards at least once on the lower bar, then he shall receive 1 extra point.

# 5\. Tiebreakers

5.1 If at the end of the event there is a tie for first place, the players who are tied with the highest score will play one additional show of “Slime Survivors” to determine the winner. The winner of the tiebreaker will receive one (1) point.

5.2 In the case that the final meant to decide the tiebreaker is “Hexagone” or “Thin Ice”, that game will not be valid. All competitors will have to jump to the slime as soon as the game begins and a new game will be started. This process will be repeated until the competitors get a final that is not any of the ones mentioned in the first sentence of this paragraph.

5.3 If the number of players who are tied is less than 5, the organizers or other players designated by them will join the lobby just for the purpose of reaching the minimum player count required to start the show. These players will then eliminate themselves as soon as possible, leaving only those players who are tied in contention for first place.

5.4 Spectators can be allowed into the tiebreaker game at the Referee’s criterium. Players or users admitted as spectators will have to jump to the slime as soon as the round starts.The tiebreaker officially begins when all the spectators have thrown themselves into the slime.

5.5 The winner will be decided in one of the finals featured on “Slime Survivors”, with the exceptions made on point 5.2, or if the number of players or spectators is enough for it to happen, in a survival round, provided the game should assign the party to one of them. In the case of survival rounds the last of the contestants left alive will be the winner, no matter if they make it to the end of the round or not. If both contestants make it to the end of the round, the game will be decided in a final.

# 6\. Timeouts

Timeouts will give zero points for the show in which the timeout occurred to all players participating in said timeouts. This is valid for all finals, including Jump Showdown.

# 7\. Servers

Servers can be either Europe or US East. This will be decided by the event organizers and communicated to the players via the assigned text chat channel.

# 8\. Disconnections and Game Crashes

8.1 As a general rule, no action will be taken in the case of disconnections and game crashes, except in the following cases:

1) When all remaining players are disconnected due to the current show randomly selecting a round that no longer exists within the game;  
2) When the disconnection or crash is suffered by two players or more simultaneously;  
3) When the disconnection or crash happens during the last round and impacts the chances of a player who still had possibilities of winning the event for that week;  
4) When the disconnection or crash happens during tiebreakers.

In the case of (a), the show will be restarted with **all** players in attendance at that point, no matter which players had been eliminated up to that point in the show.

In all of the other exceptional cases, (b) to (d), the same show will be played again, featuring only the players who were still alive at that moment and those players who suffered the disconnection or crash. The result of the show during which the disconnection or crash occurred will be deemed invalid, having no impact on players’ scores.

8.2 Shows replayed due to these exceptional cases will only happen once per player on the same weekly event.

8.3 Proof about disconnections (a screenshot or video) is required. If it is not provided by the player, the Referee has the right to opt for not replaying the playlist.

# 9\. Referee’s Rights

In any circumstance of the game that hasn’t been referred to in this document, the Referee will have the right to take the decision he judges best according to justice and common sense. The players will have the right to show him factual evidence if they think he is making a wrong decision, but won’t have the right to protest in case of disagreement.

# 10\. No Prize

No prizes or rewards, monetary or otherwise, will be issued to any participants of the event. Players should participate only for the enjoyment of playing with like-minded players and for the honour of winning.
